Why Exam Photo Upload Fails — And How to Fix It

Exam portals reject uploads for three reasons: wrong dimensions, a file larger than the limit, or a file smaller than the minimum (IBPS wants a 20–50 KB photo and a 10–20 KB signature). A phone photo is 2–5 MB — over 100× too large. This tool handles both: select your exam, upload any photo, and it resizes to exact pixels while compressing to fit the KB limit.

Signature tips: Sign on white paper with a black pen, photograph in good light, crop tightly. If the file is over the limit, lower the quality; if it is under the minimum, the tool raises it automatically without changing the picture.

Files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server — all processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API.

Why exam photo uploads fail and how to fix them

Online exam forms such as UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET and JEE usually reject a photo or signature for one of four reasons: the pixel dimensions are wrong, the file is larger than the allowed size, the format is not accepted (most want JPG or JPEG), or the image does not follow the rules for background, face position and signature. A phone camera photo is often 2 to 5 MB, far larger than the few kilobytes most portals allow.

This tool resizes your photo or signature to the dimensions you choose and compresses it to stay under a size limit such as 20 KB or 50 KB. It runs in your browser, so your photo is not uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose the exam and whether you are resizing a photo or a signature.
  2. Upload your image in JPG or PNG format.
  3. Drag the picture inside the frame and zoom so your face (or signature) sits where you want it.
  4. Check the width, height and KB limits. Notice gives cm? Switch Units to cm and enter the DPI; the tool works out the pixels.
  5. Press Resize and Download, then confirm the file size and preview before uploading to the exam portal.

Good to know

  • Exam requirements change every year. Always check the dimensions and size limits in the latest official notification and use the values it gives.
  • Photos usually need a plain light background, a clear front-facing face and no cap or dark glasses.
  • Signatures are normally written in black or blue ink on plain white paper and then photographed or scanned.
  • Keep the original image and save the resized file under a clear name.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Resizing and compression happen inside your browser, and nothing is stored.

What if the file is still too large?

Lower the quality setting or choose a smaller maximum size, then resize again.

Are the presets always up to date?

They are provided for convenience. Exam bodies can change rules, so the official notification is the final authority.
