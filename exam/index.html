Why Exam Photo Upload Fails — And How to Fix It

Government exam portals (UPSC, SSC, NTA NEET) reject uploads for two reasons: wrong pixel dimensions (SSC CGL needs exactly 100×120px) or file size too large (SSC allows only 4–12 KB). A phone photo is 2–5 MB — over 100× too large. This tool handles both: select your exam, upload any photo, and it resizes to exact pixels while compressing to fit the KB limit.

Signature tips: Sign on white paper with a black pen, photograph in good light, crop tightly. If size still exceeds the limit, reduce JPEG quality to 60–70%.

Files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server — all processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API.
