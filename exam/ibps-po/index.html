The Handwritten Declaration Text

Write this in English, in your own handwriting, then scan or photograph it:

"I, _______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required."

If it is written by someone else or in another language, the application is treated as invalid.

Live Photo During Registration

As well as the uploaded photo, IBPS asks you to capture a live photo with a webcam, or with your phone through a QR code shown on the form. Sit against a light, preferably white background with good light, look straight at the camera, and do not wear a cap, coloured glasses or a mask.

Scanning Settings

  • Scanner resolution at least 200 DPI, set to true colour.
  • Crop close to the edge of the photo, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  • Save as JPG/JPEG. If a file is too large, lower the DPI or colours while scanning, or use the resizer below.

Other Files

For the thumb impression and the declaration, use Resize Image with the sizes above, then check the KB. For the certificate PDF, use Compress PDF to 500 KB.

Why Uploads Get Rejected

  • Too large: phone photos are 2–5 MB; the limit is a few KB.
  • Too small: files under the minimum (for example below 10 KB or 20 KB) are rejected as well. Our resizer raises the size to the minimum without changing the picture.
  • Wrong format: save as JPG/JPEG unless the notice says otherwise.
  • Unclear image: blurred, dark or tiny images and signatures are rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the photo size for IBPS PO 2026?

A JPG/JPEG of 200 × 230 pixels (preferred) and 20 KB to 50 KB, per the CRP PO/MT-XVI notice. You also capture a live photo by webcam or phone during registration.

What is the signature size for IBPS PO 2026?

140 × 60 pixels (preferred) and 10 KB to 20 KB, JPG/JPEG, signed in black ink on white paper and not in capital letters.

My signature file is below 10 KB. What do I do?

Portals reject files below the minimum. The NxtCraft exam resizer raises the file to the minimum automatically without changing the picture, so it uploads correctly.

What are the left thumb and declaration sizes?

Left thumb impression: 240 × 240 px at 200 DPI, 20–50 KB. Handwritten declaration: 800 × 400 px at 200 DPI, 50–100 KB. Both JPG/JPEG.
