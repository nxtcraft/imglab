Older Notices Said 300 KB

Several UPSC notices from 2022 to 2025 allowed up to 300 KB per file. The 2026 notices (CAPF (AC) 2026, Combined Medical Services 2026) set the photo at 20–200 KB and the signature at 20–100 KB. Use the limits printed in the notice for your own exam, since UPSC states them in each one.

Making the Files

  • Scan or photograph the photo and the signature sheet in good light.
  • Save as JPG. UPSC's online FAQ suggests tools such as MS Paint or IrfanView; our resizer below does the same in the browser.
  • Keep both files above 20 KB: the portal rejects files below the minimum too.

Why Uploads Get Rejected

  • Too large: phone photos are 2–5 MB; the limit is a few KB.
  • Too small: files under the minimum (for example below 10 KB or 20 KB) are rejected as well. Our resizer raises the size to the minimum without changing the picture.
  • Wrong format: save as JPG/JPEG unless the notice says otherwise.
  • Unclear image: blurred, dark or tiny images and signatures are rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UPSC photo size in 2026?

In the 2026 notices (CAPF (AC), CMS), the photo must be a JPG of 20 KB to 200 KB. UPSC's online FAQ gives 350 × 350 px minimum and 1000 × 1000 px maximum.

What is the UPSC signature size in 2026?

A JPG of 20 KB to 100 KB in the 2026 notices. The CAPF 2026 notice asks you to sign three times, one below the other, on white paper in black ink.

Is the UPSC limit 300 KB?

Some older notices (2022–2025) allowed 300 KB. The 2026 notices lowered it to 200 KB for the photo and 100 KB for the signature. Follow the notice for your exam.
