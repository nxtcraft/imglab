How to Take the Live Photo

  • Find a place with good light and a plain background.
  • Keep the camera at eye level and look straight ahead.
  • Keep your whole face inside the frame shown on screen, neither too close nor too far.
  • Do not wear a cap, mask or glasses/spectacles.
  • If your webcam does not work, scan the QR code on the Upload Documents page to capture the photo with your phone.
Poor-quality, tiny, blurred or side-facing photos are rejected. Do not take a picture of an existing photograph; such applications are rejected.

Signature Size: 6 cm or 4 cm?

Paragraph 9.6 of the notice gives the signature as about 6.0 cm wide × 2.0 cm high, while the online-application annexure mentions about 4.0 cm wide. The file size, 10–20 KB in JPG, is the same in both places. Our preset makes a wide 300 × 100 px signature that fits the 10–20 KB range.

Aadhaar Authentication

The notice says applications submitted with Aadhaar-based authentication will not be rejected on the ground that the photo or signature does not meet the standards.

Why Uploads Get Rejected

  • Too large: phone photos are 2–5 MB; the limit is a few KB.
  • Too small: files under the minimum (for example below 10 KB or 20 KB) are rejected as well. Our resizer raises the size to the minimum without changing the picture.
  • Wrong format: save as JPG/JPEG unless the notice says otherwise.
  • Unclear image: blurred, dark or tiny images and signatures are rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to upload a photo for SSC CGL 2026?

No. The 2026 form captures a live photo through your webcam or phone. You do not need an existing photograph, and photographing an old photo leads to rejection.

What is the SSC CGL 2026 signature size?

A JPG/JPEG of 10 KB to 20 KB, about 6.0 cm wide and 2.0 cm high (paragraph 9.6 of the notice).

Can I wear glasses for the SSC live photo?

No. The notice says not to wear a cap, mask or glasses/spectacles while capturing the photo.

My webcam is not working. What can I do?

Use the QR code on the Upload Documents page to capture the live photo with your phone.
